# GSMG.IO 5 BTC puzzle — every creator hint, in order

Everything the puzzle's creator (`Jrk Bgrt`) said that bears on solving, in
chronological order.

## Provenance

Extracted from a 51,177-message Telegram export by matching the sender field, then
excluding reply/forward headers (their sender field carries an embedded date), which
leaves **411 messages authored by the creator**, 2019-04-20 → 2026-05-28.

All times are **UTC**, converted from the export's UTC−05:00. The export renders
local time, so any list quoting these in local time will be five hours off.

Two caveats to carry:

- **The creator retroactively edited 35.4% of his 2019–2022 messages** two or more
  years later, against a 3.7% baseline for other participants, concentrated on the
  hint-bearing posts. A message's current text is not necessarily its text as posted.
- Statements dated **2026-07-12** and **2026-07-16** appear in curated summaries
  (*"My close friends have the best chance of solving it"*, *"The '5' btc was never
  the actual prize"*, *"Lately, I'm working with many NOTES"*) but are **not in this
  export**, which ends 2026-05-28. They are marked second-hand below and were not
  verified here.
