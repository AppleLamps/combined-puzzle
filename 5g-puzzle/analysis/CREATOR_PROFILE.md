# The creator, from his own words

Built from all 521 messages posted by `Jrk Bgrt` (user9815232) in the GSMG
Puzzle Solvers Telegram, 2019-04-20 to 2026-09-08. Message ids in brackets.

**Why this document exists.** In seven years the creator labelled exactly one
statement as a hint:

> "My close friends have the best chance of solving it (a few tried). But they
> don't have the skills some of you do." [#66573]
> "**NOTE: that is a hint.**" [#66574]

Read plainly: the remaining step needs personal context, not cryptanalysis. The
people best placed lack the technical skill entirely. That is a claim about
*recognition* — knowing which object he would reach for — and it is the one
thing no amount of searching produces. This profile is an attempt to supply it.

**Standing caveat.** Nothing here is evidence and nothing here authenticates
anything. It is character reconstruction from self-report, and roughly half his
early messages carry edit timestamps years later, so even the quotations are
"as they now read". Treat every inference as a prior for choosing what to try,
never as a reason to accept a result.

## 3. How he builds

**Wordplay is the primitive, not mathematics.** Every authenticated stage turns
on a pun or a substitution: `HSM` = "**H**ow **s**o **m**ate", SafeNet/Luna as
"2name"/"3Moon", `SalPhaseIon` ≈ salvation, `shabef` = sha + 2·5·6, "beautiful
strategic position" → Beaufort, "one for one, four for one" → EBCDIC 1141. He
never once requires original mathematics. He requires that you hear the joke.

**He works in bursts and ships the result unchecked.**

> "I have these moment where I get into a frenzy and zone in with 800%. A day
> later I can hardly understand what I have done. And that is not a joke." [#66561]
> "A. I rushed (t)it." [#70311]
> — agreeing he "spent two sloppy days throwing one together" [#70377]

He verified the chain three times and still shipped `givetit` [#871]. Typos are
his signature failure and he says outright they carry no meaning [#1806, #3345].
**Do not read significance into irregularity.** He is not that careful.

**Register.** Answers in one to three words — "Nobody knows", "No hints 🤡",
"🤐", "Iykyk", "Correct." Deflects with emoji. Apologises a lot. Warm to
regulars, blunt with beggars. Asked whether he is a one or a zero: *"I prefer
superpositions"* [#23159].

## 6. What I would take from this

1. **He is not testing cryptanalysis.** Every stage he built rewards hearing a
   pun. Seven years of increasingly heroic mathematics is a category error, and
   his one labelled hint says so in as many words.
2. **The last step is a recognition, not a computation.** "2 hours max" after
   reaching it means the work after recognition is trivial. All the difficulty
   is in the noticing.
3. **The unused reference list in §4 is the actual search space** — small,
   personal, and enumerable, unlike the passphrase space, which is now closed.
4. **He expected the finder to understand the point** before getting the coins.
   Anyone approaching this purely as 1.25 BTC is, by his own framing, not the
   person he wrote it for.
