# What the group missed (2019–2026)

**Question:** If GSMG is still unsolved after seven years, what did this chat actually fail to do?  
**Method:** Jrk Bgrt’s own messages in the export, set against what the group spent volume on. Not a private-key derivation.  
**Status:** First draft over-identified an extra-door finish line. The **critical review** immediately below is the current claim.

The prize wallet is still funded. That is not evidence that the remaining step is a harder cipher. Jrk’s record says the opposite: the remaining step was supposed to be **obvious once a skipped branch was found**, and he could not even check whether anyone had found it.

## Critical review (what survives, what does not)

A second pass against the same export **does not fully confirm** the extra-door-as-the-finish-line thesis. Several load-bearing quotes are later edits, one cited “coordinate hint” is an April Fools post, and Jrk had already confirmed people *inside Salphaseion* in 2021. The communication-process findings are unchanged. The puzzle-direction findings need the following corrections.

### Disproved or too weak to keep as stated

**1. `{1},{4},{21}` is not a confirmed live coordinate.**  
`#6884` is dated **2021-04-01**. Same-day replies asked if it was April Fools (`#6894`). On 2021-04-18 `#7529` Jrk answered a direct “is this an actual hint?” with “Do you know what usually happens on the first of april?” He did not ratify the triple. The message was also **edited 2025-02-02**. Treating those numbers as a standing instruction was an overclaim.

**2. “Stop grinding Salph / the extra door is the unfinished finish” is too strong.**  
By 2021-03-14 Jrk was already reporting teams **in Salphaseion** (`#6491`, `#6497`) and talking as if breaking “salphation” was the expected hard work. An extra door that *nobody* had found in 2020-08 cannot be identical with Salph if people were in Salph seven months later — unless that door was the *path into* Salph for some teams, not a still-skipped last stage. 2024 `#28225` (“I found the other door, but Salph is still required”) is unverifiable, but it is the opposite of “door = skip Salph.”

If Jrk’s “next phase, 2 hours max” (`#39224`, edited only 14 seconds later — treat as contemporaneous) refers to *after that blob yields*, then “don’t do more AES” is the wrong prescription. This export cannot tell Cosmic Duality from a skipped rabbit-nest door.

### Revised claim

The group **did** miss something in 2020: Jrk said an extra door on the first piece was unfound, and the chat stayed on the obvious 3.2 track. That is **not** sufficient to say the 2023–2026 Salph/FAED/96-state grind is the wrong object. Jrk treated Salph as a real, reached stage whose break should feel like “salvation,” told them in Jan 2023 they were “getting close” (`#8352`, unedited) while they were documenting those stages, and named **yinyang** as a later short phase that, by his 2025 remark, still had not happened.

A fairer statement of the miss:

1. **Unclosed order.** Extra door, leftover 3.2 AES, Salph remainder, and Cosmic Duality/yinyang were never put in a single authenticated sequence. The chat still argues which one blocks which (`#71205`).
2. **Unverifiable private finds** instead of hashed in-channel closure.
3. **Hint text that moves.** Later edits of old Jrk posts make a stable board impossible if people treat the 2026 wording as 2020 law.
4. **Yinyang not reached (per Jrk, 2025),** whatever object it is. A seven-year AES/key-search stall is the wrong *duration* for a phase he said should be hours — unless they are not in that phase yet.

The extra-door-only finish line is **not proven**. “We need one more FAED consumer” is also **not proven**. The export supports: **the order of remaining objects was never settled, and the named last short phase has not been entered.**

Not “one more encoding of FAED.” Also not “abandon Salph and only hunt `{1},{4},{21}`” (that triple is April Fools-contaminated). The miss is **unsettled sequence + no hashed closure + edited hint canon.**

## Five things the group did wrong

### 1. They promoted the last visible ciphertext to “the whole remaining puzzle”

Jrk noticed this as early as 2020-02-25: most people already saw 3.2 as the last part, and “nobody found the direction.” Salphaseion then absorbed that role. On the last day of the export (`#71205`) the chat is still arguing whether unsolved 3.2 is why Salph fails.

That debate can stay open forever because **Jrk never posted a single remaining-stage order** that this export can show as unedited and complete. He named an extra door (2020), Salph as a reached hard phase (2021), and yinyang as a later short phase (2023/2025).

**Confidence: high** that order is unset (mention spans are mechanical). **Low–medium** that Salph/FAED is therefore the wrong work.

### 2. They never closed the extra-door branch in-channel

Jrk said it in plain language in **unedited** `#4590` (2020-08-02): nobody found the extra door. Unedited `#7914` (2021-12-02) still says there is another door. The 2020 tiny hint (`#1710`, quoted identically in 2020 `#3388`) points at piece 1 / rabbit nest.

That does **not** prove the door is still the last unfinished stage. People were in Salph by March 2021. `{1},{4},{21}` is not a confirmed coordinate. `#8000`’s primes/zeroing clause may be a 2026 expansion. 2024 `#28225` claims a door find that still required Salph.

**Confidence: high** that this chat never published a hashed extra-door proof. **Low** that skipping Salph to hunt only piece 1 is the intended remaining work.

### 3. They used “hardest part is done” to justify more grind, not less

`#8796` plus `#9599` plus `#9607` together read: you already passed the hard ciphers; you have the info; the last named step (yinyang) is short.

The chat read it as: Salph remainder “has to be easier,” so keep decoding FAED (`#10131`). Easier-than-3.2 does not mean “another four years of matrix products.” It matches “same day / two hours” once the correct phase is actually reached.

**Confidence: high** on the misreading (explicit quotes). **Medium** on which object yinyang is; Jrk never posted a picture, and `#39237` only calls it “the next phase.”

### 4. They brute-forced a missing *rule* as if it were missing *entropy*

`#69940` (2026) reports hundreds of millions of candidate keys checked against the known public key: yinyang bit-masks, base-96 orderings, zeroing rules, Half/Better-Half, XOR-triangle, π-selectors — all negative.

That is the correct test for a key, and the wrong response to Jrk’s own last-hint design. He said the last step is a **giveaway in front of your eyes** (`#543` / `#10791`), that more hints would give it away (`#9621`), and that yinyang is hours, not compute (`#39224`). Exhausting the 96-state consumer without the extra door / yinyang is expected to return zero. It is not new information that the puzzle is unsolvable.

**Confidence: medium** that unconstrained 96-state key-search is the wrong *finish*. **Low** that it is the wrong *current work* if yinyang = Cosmic Duality AES still closed.

### 5. After 2023 they replaced the original puzzle with LLM-generated side quests

The export’s own late audit (`#69939`) flags `cosmic_A` / XOR-triangle operands that never existed in published pages. Frequency dashboards of a **4×21** grid were offered as the **4×24 = 96-state** object. `#71223`: “can we pls stop putting LLM-Books in here, not helpful.”

This is the same failure as (1)–(4) at higher volume: new artifacts that Jrk never named, crowding out the extra-door and yinyang items he did name.

**Confidence: high** for 2025–2026. The 2019–2022 stall does not need LLMs to explain it; the extra door was already unfound in 2020.

## What this is not

- **Not** “the puzzle was a scam.” Jrk, 2019-05-14 `#851`: not unsolvable. 2023-01-12 `#8360`: appears unsolvable, isn’t. 2026-05-28 `#63957`: still valid. 2023-08-06 `#9621`: he would rather drop a giveaway than leave a scam suspicion.
- **Not** “need gsmg.io online.” 2023-11-24 `#16624`: internet not required given then-current knowledge. 2021-01-21 `#5717`: some parts may not need internet.
- **Not** a new AES password for leftover 3.2 **or** a new Bifid key for FAED, unless that operation is what the extra-door / prime / zeroing instructions actually produce. Those two blobs have been the default work queue for five years without a wallet move.
- **Not** Jrk’s 2026-07-12 drinking-session lines (“5 btc was never the actual prize,” “some already found it”) as operational clues. He flags alcohol in the same threads; treat them as noise unless independently confirmed.

## What to do with this (without another FAED script)

1. **Build a board that records edit dates.** Do not treat 2026 wording of `#8000` / `#6884` as 2021 law.
2. **Require SHA-256 of any extra-door or yinyang claim** in-channel (2019 proof bar). Private “I found it” does not close the branch.
3. **Keep leftover 3.2, Salph remainder, and Cosmic Duality as three open objects** until Jrk or a hashed decrypt orders them. Do not collapse them into one FAED script.
4. **Treat yinyang as unreached (Jrk 2025).** If a method does not look like a same-day finish, it is not that phase yet — Cosmic Duality AES is a live candidate, not a dismissed one.
5. **Stop promoting heatmap / XOR / URL-LSB artifacts** that are not in the 2023 roadmap string.

This does not output a key. The first draft over-identified the extra door as the whole miss; the review keeps **unsettled order + unreached yinyang + moving hint text.**
