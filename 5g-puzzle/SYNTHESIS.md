## What does not hold, or is weaker than it sounds

**“Seven intertwined passwords = interleave the stage secrets.”**  
Intertwined in this puzzle already has a published meaning: XOR of seven SHA-256s as the Cosmic Duality KDF password. That works. Interleaving/concat/hex of “all seven stage secrets” against leftover 3.2 (739 assemblies / 13,302 decryptions, zero hits) is now **closed**. Remaining “which 7 / what stride” is unbounded. Do not continue that family.

**Left door = Cosmic Duality, right door = Salphaseion, and that *is* yinyang.**  
A consistent fan mapping. Jrk still said yinyang **unreached** in 2025 *after* people were in Salph (2021) and after public GitHub stages (2023 `#8352`). Cosmic Duality AES is already open and is not a same-day finish. The two-door split is not proven to be two leftover AES passwords.

**“5 BTC was never the actual prize.”**  
Jrk drinking-session lines from 2026 are **unsafe canon** (`WHAT_THE_GROUP_MISSED.md`). The VIC sentence about half/better half is real puzzle text; it does not retire `1GSMG`.

**“enter” has never been executed.**  
`enter` was already a packet-1 leftover-3.2 password (raw + sha256hex). Negative. What was *not* done is treating `matrixsumlist` as **sum the matrix, then enter that list** — packet 4.

**Neo’s passport date as a missing key component.**  
Jrk `#8048` (2021-12-31) is a refusal to give personal dates, with the movie passport as the dodge. It is a named date, not a confirmed operand. Frozen and tested in packet 4; not a reason to grow a calendar.

**140.**  
Named in the Architect plaintext. Matched on the original 14×14 grid (majority 86/86/15/9, and `1²+…+7²=140`). Not an AES password (`password_list_140.json`). Not a Cosmic Duality colour-diff (ClearType). Consumed as a length on the spiral: drop `gsmg.io` (7 chars = 56 cells) → 140 bits = `/theseedisplanted` + `0000`. That is the main door with the hostname stripped, not a second door. `packet/RESULTS_140.md`, `packet/RESULTS_140_LENGTH.md`.

**Extra door.**  
Grey at spiral 164 is authored. As a password, closed. As a length-140 cut, it sits inside the 140 and is not isolated. As a read instruction (half-step, bit insert/flip/drop, stride-4, stream heads), closed — 72 takes, no second path (`packet/RESULTS_FEFEFE_READ.md`). Do not grow the grid-encoding list.
