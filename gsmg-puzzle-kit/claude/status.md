## Important corrections

- L83's fixed-prefix fit does not globally exclude L84. Two L84 selection fits
  remain in the reported audit; one-per-byte is nonunique. None of the five
  audited branches gives a fully supported chronological continuation.
- FAED and the DBBI residue are compatible with a filler model; their purpose
  and lack of other payload are not proven.
- Authenticity does not determine mandatory envelope use, order or contents.
  `Salted__` and length do not identify the locked cipher. CBC plaintext-length
  bounds are conditional.
- The unsigned coordinate has an independently documented historical SafeNet
  address and a current building-scale match. Its minus sign is unresolved;
  current geometry does not verify the historical footprint.
- Genesis part 6 is exactly 140 characters of Bitcoin comment/printer text.
  “Hundred fourty” is best read in a Fresco/community/time context, but its exact
  number and any binding to the genesis value or “code you carry” remain open.
- Category-aware counts do not establish a 23/16/7 selection. Prime reinsertion
  may already be represented by DBBI. Ownership language is not a key-split rule.
- Earlier answers use stage-specific case and spacing. Joint assembly and the
  two SHA/`enter`/`anstoo` models remain unresolved.
