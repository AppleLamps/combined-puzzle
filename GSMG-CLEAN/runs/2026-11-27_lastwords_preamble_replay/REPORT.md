# Last words before the Terminal choice

All 619 candidates were tested on the three locked envelopes under the pinned gsmg profile. Of 1,857 decrypts, nine passed strict PKCS#7. Each had one byte of padding. All nine plaintexts were saved and scanned with cc.find_keys against Half and Better Half. None contained coherent text or a matching key. An independent AES-CBC implementation reproduced all nine byte for byte.

## Candidate construction

The seven requested bases were PRE, DIG, PRE+DIG, DIG+PRE, PRE+RID, DIG+RID, and RID+DIG. The three combinations PRE+DIG, DIG+PRE, and PRE+RID extend the base list in lastwords.py as requested. The generator reuses the seven normalization forms and eleven EXTRA tokens from that file, with both prefix and suffix placement. It produced 620 distinct strings before exclusion. Only the exact string yinyang occurred in the previous run, leaving 619. The first entry is the empty string, generated by removing digits from DIG. The 176 retained candidates built with yinyang, yinyang479, or 479yinyang carry solver-only tags. Creator #39237 identifies yinyang as the next phase.
