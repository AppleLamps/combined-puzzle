# 18 - Reading the labels as riddles

**Status (2026-11-10): UNSOLVED.** This file changes direction and records what the creator's own
messages say about two of the page's phrases. It proposes no password, generates no candidates and
touches no envelope. Every quotation was re-read in `telegram/ChatExport_2026-09-13_result.json`.

## 1. Direction: stop decoding DBBI and FAED for a password

From here on the two unread fields are treated as structure that is understood as far as it goes, not
as the place the password is hidden.

Why:

- FAED has no letter-level structure and is not one object (16 section 5). Nothing has ever been read
  out of it.
- DBBI is a second copy of the poster's colour sequence on prime cells. Three sealed readings of its
  ordinary cells were null (16 section 6), on top of the two imported from the source branch (17
  section 2).
- Asked directly whether DBBI can be decrypted, the creator did not say yes: "No hints" (#9611,
  2023-08-06, one minute after the question in #9610; it carries no reply link).
- In every solved stage the password was a plain-language answer to a riddle. The ciphers inside the
  plaintexts delivered messages and never produced a password (01 sections 2 to 5).

What this does not say: that the fields are filler. That is unproven and stays open (16 section 7).

## 2. "our first hint is your last command"

The page says `shabefourfirsthintisyourlastcommand`. The open question has always been which hint is
"our first hint". The export answers it in the creator's own words.

| Message | Date | Text | Context |
|---|---|---|---|
| #11248 | 2023-08-22 | "Follow the white rabbit" | Forwarded by a solver from a private exchange. The solver's question, forwarded as #11247: "what was the actual first hint to the gsmg puzzle". Follow-up #11249: "That was years ago" |
| #25550, #33326, #46586 | 2024 to 2025 | "Follow the white rabbit. 😉" | Forwards of one creator reply. In #33325 and #33326 it is shown answering "when hint?" |
| #881 | 2019-05-18 | "we'll release a tiny hint at the start of 2020, that will be the final hint" | So the January 2020 poem was never called the first hint; it was announced as the last |
| #20224 | 2024-01-26 | "🤐" | Reply to #20222, a solver asking for help with exactly this phrase |

Two things outside the chat agree. The poster's file name is `follow_the_white_rabbit.png`, and the
site's root page, as relaunched in 2026, ends "Follow the white rabbit" (17 section 5).

So the phrase most plausibly reads: **the last thing you do is the thing we told you first, follow
the white rabbit.** That makes it a statement about the final step, in line with the creator's "very
last step is a true giveaway" (#8446). It is weaker evidence for the alternative that solvers usually
assume, that "first hint" means the Decentraland audio (`HASHTHETEXT`), because the creator was asked
the question and gave a different answer.

Three ways to read "follow the white rabbit" as a last step. None is tested, and none is preferred.

1. **Do the first step again.** The first step was reading a grid along a spiral that ends at the
   rabbit. On this reading the final material is a grid, and the giveaway is that it is read the way
   the poster was.
2. **Go back to the poster.** The January 2020 poem says the same thing in other words ("Go back to
   the first puzzle piece ... the rabbits nest may contain a whole lot more"). On this reading
   something in the poster is still unused at the end. The rabbit drawing and the four surplus centre
   cells are the parts no solved step consumes (01 section 1).
3. **Continue the film's sequence.** The text on Neo's screen runs: wake up, the Matrix has you,
   follow the white rabbit, knock knock. Phase 3.2 used the first two lines (its opening sentence and
   the Beaufort key) and the poster is the third. The fourth is the only one the puzzle has not used,
   and it is about a door.

## 3. "The theory of everything" is a running joke, not a riddle

Several documents, and my own summary of 2026-11-10, treated this line as a hint:

> "Focussing on the theory of everything is also still a valid path to reaching the private key."
> (#8354, 2023-01-12)

In context it is the punchline of an exchange with one solver:

It says that discovering new physics would also get you the key, which is a joke about how hard the
puzzle is. It names no object in the puzzle. Readings built on it should be dropped, including the
chain in `puzle/` that derives a surname from the phrase and the entries in `5g-puzzle/` and the
dossiers that list it as a live hint.

What the same hour of chat does contain is serious: "I checked a certain GitHub yesterday, for whoever
set that up, nicely done. You guys are really getting close." (#8352). At that date the write-up had
the four decoded labels and nothing more.

## 4. What the puzzle says as a whole

Read in order, the solved stages make an argument, and the creator's later messages say the argument
was the point.

| Stage | What it says |
|---|---|
| Phase 1, the song | Something grows through what looks like concrete |
| Phase 2 | The Merovingian's line: causality is the only constant, and choice is an illusion kept by those with power |
| Phase 2.1 | The keepers of the keys that protect today's digital powers guard them poorly; a ruler who touched the highest power over money died for it; the Bitcoin genesis message about bank bailouts; a chess position in which one side is forced to move |
| Phase 3 | "What if the Merovingian is wrong": the future is ours (Fresco), forever is one second, and there is a limit to what can be known |
| Phase 3.2 | The Architect, rewritten: take to heart what the wise man above hinted at, and "help us build it instead of ... hunting for worthless prizes" |
| SalPhaseIon | Named for the door to "the salvation of Zion"; its companion is headed Cosmic Duality, and the book of that name says each half holds the seed of its opposite |

The direction is from control to choice: money and keys held by the powerful, then the claim that
this is not fixed, then an invitation to build something else. The first page's address, "the seed is
planted", and the last page's yin and yang say the same thing at each end.

The creator on what the prize is:

- "Are you really looking for just the btc...?" (#8774, 2023-08-03). Asked whether that was the hint:
  "Might be. Wish I had said nothing though." (#8787)
- "A private key, some 'obscure' intel, or what I hope most of you have discovered by now, the most
  obvious reason to make sure you hold on to Bitcoin" (#24627, 2024-04-19)
- "I simply liked Jacque's ideas a lot. I have techy friends building communities around that idea
  today" (#26875, 2024-09-19)
- "I held quite a secret in my head which I seriously wanted to share with the planet... for those who
  can understand what I meant" (#66592, 2026-07-12), and "The '5' btc was never the actual prize"
  (#66593)
- "My close friends have the best chance of solving it (a few tried). But they don't have the skills
  some of you do. NOTE: that is a hint." (#66573, #66574)

What follows from this, and what does not:

- The idea is not hidden. It is the plain content of Phases 2 to 3.2. "Some already found it" (#66600)
  can be true of the idea without anyone having opened an envelope.
- The friends hint describes a division of labour. The early stages need technical skill; the last one
  needs someone who knows how he thinks. That fits a final answer that is a short phrase in his own
  idiom, and it fits section 2: he told people what the first hint was when they asked.
- None of this yields the answer. It narrows the kind of answer to expect: something stated in the
  puzzle's own words, short, and obvious in hindsight.

## 5. Limits

- This file interprets; it does not test. Under the working rules (16 section 2) nothing here may be
  tried against an envelope, because a riddle answer has no self-check short of the envelope itself.
  Whether a short, fixed list of riddle answers should be allowed through that gate is a decision for
  the owner of the repository, not something this file assumes.
- The creator is a private person. Nothing here draws on anything but his public messages in the
  solvers' group, and no attempt was made to learn more about him.
- Section 2 rests on one forwarded private exchange for its strongest item. The repeated public
  "Follow the white rabbit" replies, the file name and the site text support it, but none of them is
  the creator saying "this is what the label means".
