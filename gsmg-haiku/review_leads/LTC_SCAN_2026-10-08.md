**Result: not completed. No match to the target.** The scan was stopped at the user's request after 257 of 2,176 addresses. No private key was recovered.

Target: hash160 `a9553269572a317e39f0f518cb87c1a0ee1dbae4` (1GSMG1JC9wtdSwfwApgj2xcmJPAwx7prBe). The question was whether the same hash160 could be a Litecoin P2PKH (version 0x30) address, so the scan derived keys from candidate phrases and checked the matching addresses on both chains.

## Why it stopped

Progress was logged at 100 and 200 addresses. The first version (`ltc_scan.py`) printed nothing for about 12 minutes because Python buffered stdout and the script wrote only at the end. The replacement `ltc_scan2.py` uses `python -u`, 8 worker threads, progress every 100 addresses, and per-address JSONL output. At about 0.6 to 0.8 lookups per second, the remaining ~1,919 addresses would take about 40 to 55 minutes.

The user asked for the run to end. Stopping the task did not stop the Python process: at the review on 2026-10-08 it was still running (PID 8748, `python -u -I ltc_scan2.py`) and had written 257 results. It was terminated with `taskkill` at 23:38. The final count is 257 lines, and the results file was not written after that.
